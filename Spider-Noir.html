Spider-Noir

Set in a gritty, Great Depression-era New York, the show centers on Ben Reilly, an aging gumshoe struggling to balance his cynicism with his innate spider-powers. Though he attempted to hang up his mask and step away from being "The Spider" after failing to save his girlfriend, Ruby, he soon finds himself on a collision course with his old nemesis, the ruthless mob boss Silvermane (Brendan Gleeson).

As Reilly investigates a series of seemingly straightforward mysteries, he becomes entangled in a dangerous web involving political corruption, an enigmatic femme fatale, and bizarre body-horror experiments. The investigation forces Reilly to confront the dark reality of his own origins—which tie back to a World War I encounter with twisted German genetic experiments.
